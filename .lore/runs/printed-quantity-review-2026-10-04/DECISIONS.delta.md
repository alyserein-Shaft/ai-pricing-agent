- [DEC-2026-10-04-PQRA-BARENUMBER] A PRINTED NUMBER ON ITS OWN IS NEVER QUANTITY AUTHORITY -- THIS IS THE CENTRAL RULE OF THE SLICE. Live evidence forced this: every one of the 17 `quantities` proposals in the corpus is self-flagged `hard_review_reasons: ["AI_VISUAL_FINDING_REQUIRES_ENGINEER_CONFIRMATION","QUANTITY_DEVICE_ASSOCIATION_UNKNOWN"]`, i.e. the engine read the numeral but cannot say WHICH DEVICE it counts, and it stores no `normalized_value` and no `bounding_box` ("a language model's output is not a reliable pixel coordinate; never fabricate one"). Consequently a plain `approve` of such a candidate sets `review_status=Verified` while leaving `corrected_value` NULL, binding no device class at all. The bridge REFUSES that with `QUANTITY_ADJUDICATION_MISSING` and names the fix (use the review path's `correct` action). Only `correct` can carry authority, and only as a full adjudication `{quantity, deviceClass, countMethod, printedText}` recorded in the existing audit trail. `approve` -> no authority; `reject`/`Conflict`/`Needs Review` -> `REVIEW_NOT_AUTHORISED`; `correct` with a consistent adjudication -> eligible. This is what "do not ask the human to approve a bare number" means in code. #added:2026-10-04
- [DEC-2026-10-04-PQRA-PRINTEDTEXT] THE ADJUDICATED NUMBER MUST BE THE NUMERAL ACTUALLY PRINTED; THIS MAKES THE "6 x 2 = 12" SHORTCUT UNREACHABLE. `validatePrintedQuantityAdjudication` extracts every integer literal from the reviewer's `printedText` and requires the asserted quantity to be reconcilable with it: exactly one numeral -> quantity must equal it; two or more numerals -> permitted ONLY as `COMPONENT_CELL_SUM` whose quantity equals their product. So `12` against printed `"2 Nos"` is `PRINTED_QUANTITY_TEXT_MISMATCH` (the recognition-6 shortcut in disguise), `"6 x 2 Nos"` as `PRINTED_CELL` is `PRINTED_QUANTITY_TEXT_AMBIGUOUS`, `COMPONENT_SUM_WITHOUT_COMPONENTS` rejects a sum declared over a single printed numeral (the recognizer's 6 cannot supply the missing printed factor), and printed text with no numeral at all (`"Nil"`) is refused as a vocabulary gap rather than guessed. The recognition barrier is re-applied on `countMethod` BEFORE the vocabulary test (`RECOGNITION_COUNT_METHOD_REFUSED` for OCCURRENCE/SYMBOL_COUNT/PLOTTED/RECOGNITION) so a future vocabulary addition cannot leak through the review path either. #added:2026-10-04
