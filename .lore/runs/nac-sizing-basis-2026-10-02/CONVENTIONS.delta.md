- [CONV-2026-10-02-4b7e] AGGREGATE A QUANTITY IN SQL, NOT IN JAVASCRIPT, WHEN THE COLUMN MAY BE STORED AS TEXT. `boq_items.numeric_quantity` is TEXT for every loop-powered row, so `rows.reduce((a,r)=>a+r.numeric_quantity,0)` concatenates strings and coerces to a garbage magnitude (9.7e+99 observed), while SQL `SUM(COALESCE(numeric_quantity,0))` coerces per-row and returns 3,240. Worse, the garbage total does not look like an error. Any sizing or quantity reconciliation must aggregate in SQL, and a population that comes out NEGATIVE is the tell that description matching was substring-based rather than exact. #added:2026-10-02
- [CONV-2026-10-02-8c31] A PANEL'S PER-CIRCUIT RATING IS NOT ITS NOTIFICATION CAPACITY. The IFP-2100 offers 8 Flexput NACs at 3 A each, which arithmetically suggests 24 A, but the same datasheet states "Cannot exceed 9A total for all circuits". The 9 A total is the real constraint and it is what sizing must use. Reading only the per-circuit figure, or only the circuit count, overstates available notification power by 2.7x. When a datasheet states both a per-circuit limit and a total limit, the total governs. #added:2026-10-02
