- [DEC-2026-10-03-DQBL-NOSPLIT] THE ANTI-DOUBLE-COUNT RULE IS TWO PARTIAL UNIQUE INDEXES, WITH NO NEW COLUMN AND NO QUANTITY SPLIT. `drawing_quantity_boq_links_current_claim_idx` on `(project_id, drawing_quantity_claim_id)` WHERE current AND APPLICABLE AND Approved, and `drawing_quantity_boq_links_current_item_idx` on `(project_id, boq_item_id)` with the same predicate. Together these make APPLICABLE links a partial bijection: one claim supplies at most ONE BOQ row, and one BOQ row is supplied by at most ONE claim. **CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY: several per-floor claims CANNOT contribute to one BOQ row as summed quantities.** That case is REPRESENTABLE and auditable (the extra rows persist as PROPOSED/AMBIGUOUS, recording the conflict for review) but never flows a quantity: the reader reports `MULTIPLE_DRAWING_QUANTITY_BOQ_LINKS` and Address Demand stays blocked. An aggregate BOQ row instead needs either a re-extraction into per-floor rows (a commercial action, not an inference) or a separate governed aggregation authority; neither is in scope here. Many PROPOSED/AMBIGUOUS rows per BOQ row remain permitted by design -- they document a conflict and never consume. #added:2026-10-03

- [DEC-2026-10-03-DQBL-SHAPE] A GOVERNED CLAIM HAS TWO REAL SHAPES AND READING ONE AS THE OTHER IS A SILENT BUG. `buildQuantityClaim` returns `{ok, claim}` where `claim` has snake_case columns, a NESTED `review: {status, reviewed_by, reason}` object, NO `id` (the writer mints it) and NO `version_number`. Reading a domain object as a row makes `claim.review_status` `undefined`, which the write predicate would report as an UNAPPROVED claim -- a governed, approved claim silently refused. `normaliseClaim` now reduces either form to one flat shape and EVERY claim-reading path goes through it, so the mistake cannot be made in one place and not the other.
