- [DEC-2026-10-02-3e18] THE NAC CIRCUIT COUNT MUST BE COMPUTED PER FAMILY, NEVER BY DIVIDING TOTAL CURRENT BY THE PER-CIRCUIT LIMIT. The earlier figure of 43 circuits came from `ceil(127.890 A / 3 A)`, which divides a MIXED load by a single per-circuit limit and therefore assumes every circuit carries the same appliance. Because strobes take 10 per circuit and horn/strobes take 9, the correct per-family result is 33 + 2 + 12 = 47 circuits at worst case and 8 + 1 + 3 = 12 at the documented lower bound. Dividing a mixed total understates the circuit count by 4. Correct method: `perCircuit = floor(3000 mA / applianceCurrent)` per family, then `circuits = ceil(qty / perCircuit)` per family, then sum. #added:2026-10-02
