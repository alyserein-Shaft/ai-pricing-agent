- [CONV-2026-10-02-8b41] A LOWER BOUND MUST BE THE TRUE DOCUMENTED MINIMUM, NOT THE MINIMUM OF ONE SOUND PATTERN. The 245 mA figure used earlier is the lowest TEMPORAL HIGH value in the 135-185 cd horn/strobe range; the true minimum in that range is 232 mA (135 cd, Temporal Low). Because NFPA 72 designs normally run temporal high, 245 mA is a defensible worst-case-per-pattern value, but calling it a LOWER BOUND is wrong. The documented overall minimum at 16-33 VDC is 66 mA (15 cd, Temporal Low), and that is what a genuine lower bound must use. Name which sound pattern a figure belongs to whenever a table has a pattern axis. #added:2026-10-02
