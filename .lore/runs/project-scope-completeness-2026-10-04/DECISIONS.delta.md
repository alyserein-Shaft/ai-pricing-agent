- [DEC-2026-10-04-PSC-FOURSTATES] PER-MEMBER STATE DISTINGUISHES FOUR THINGS THAT MUST NOT COLLAPSE. `SCOPE_MEMBER_STATES` = PROVEN / CONFLICT / UNRESOLVED / MISSING. Only PROVEN carries a quantity; the other three report `null`, which is deliberately NOT zero. A conflict has evidence but no authority; a missing location has no evidence at all; an unresolved class has neither a governed meaning nor a quantity; and a reviewed GOVERNED ZERO is a real PROVEN quantity of 0.
